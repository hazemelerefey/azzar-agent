**[English Executive Summary]**

**Subject: Key Discovery Channels for Egyptian SMEs**

The research indicates that Egyptian SMEs primarily discover and utilize marketing channels through two main vectors:

1.  **Social Commerce Dominance:** SMEs heavily rely on social media platforms (Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp Business) as their primary and most effective channel for customer acquisition and direct sales.
2.  **Demand for Practical AI Tools:** There is a strong preference for practical, Arabic-first AI tools that offer immediate time-saving benefits in daily operations, such as content creation, lead tracking, and customer follow-up.
3.  **Focus on Efficiency:** Businesses are actively seeking direct, practical solutions that allow them to manage their marketing efforts efficiently without the high overhead of hiring a full-scale marketing agency.
